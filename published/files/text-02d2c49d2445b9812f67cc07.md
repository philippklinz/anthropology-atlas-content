How did a voyage to the Spice Islands become a circumnavigation? This chapter follows the expedition of 1519–1522 across the Atlantic, Pacific and Indian Ocean.

Magellan led the departure. After his death in 1521, Elcano brought Victoria back. The voyage depended on many participants and also involved violence: on Mactan, Lapulapu’s forces resisted the attack.

## Reading the map

The dashed line connects selected stations schematically. It shows neither an exact track nor a ship’s position in the selected year. Points are approximate orientation aids. Present-day countries provide orientation only; historical borders are not reconstructed here.

## Sources and limits

This is a small research draft based on consulted sources, without independent specialist review. Pigafetta’s account is supplemented by institutional interpretations. Different perspectives do not constitute a seamless history. Day labels retain the conventional Julian chronology; a separate Gregorian calendar is used for calculations.
