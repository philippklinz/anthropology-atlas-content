# Oracle Bones of Anyang

A turtle shell preserves a concern. A bronze vessel carries a name. A piece of clay preserves traces of work in a foundry. All three come from Yinxu, the remains of the last Shang capital in present-day Anyang. The stops along the Huan River lead from the questions asked at court to the making of inscribed vessels.

## Xiaotun

Would the birth of a child to Fu Hao go well? This question concerned the court in the reign of King Wu Ding, around 1250 to 1192 BCE. It appears on Ping 0247, a piece from the underside of a turtle shell. The divination tied its prediction to the expected day of birth.

For divination, people applied heat to make cracks in bones or shells. They read the cracks and recorded questions and readings in writing. In 1936, excavators unearthed more than 17,000 inscribed pieces in Pit YH127. The photograph shows the excavation of the pit. The piece about Fu Hao is catalogued at Academia Sinica as R041287.

## Fu Hao

A few hundred metres away lies tomb M5, dated to around 1200 BCE. Many bronze ritual vessels were among the objects excavated in 1976. One is a standing owl, about 46 centimetres tall. In the photograph, the two feet and the end of the tail support the vessel. Fu Hao’s name appears on the inside wall.

The name also appears on numerous other bronzes from the same tomb. Fu Hao was one of Wu Ding’s consorts. Texts from the oracle bone pit and inscriptions from the tomb connect a name recorded at court with a particular archaeological findsite.

## Xiaomintun

Bronze workshops lay west of the palace district. At Xiaomintun Southeast, the foundry remains belong to the later phases of Yinxu, roughly 1150 to 1050 BCE. Among the workshop finds was a clay block about six centimetres tall with eleven signs in three columns.

Traces of raised strokes survive in the grooves. The scholar of early writing Ondřej Škrabal interprets them as signs modelled for a casting mould. The signs could then transfer to the metal during casting. The timeline marks the approximate period of the foundry context. The date of the individual block remains open.
