# Kingdoms and Trade Networks

Research draft, 2026-11-23.

The discovery period **Königreiche und Handelsnetze / Kingdoms and Trade Networks** compares selected evidence around 1800–1000 BCE. Local dating controls the chapter windows. The group includes comparisons with regions that have their own period names and whose connections follow separate histories.

| Chapter | Evidence and question |
| --- | --- |
| Kerma and Egypt | Archaeological evidence from Nile settlements. What did buildings, objects and inscriptions preserve about interaction along the river? |
| Letters Between Royal Courts | The Assyrian letter found at Amarna and the treaty surviving at Hattusa and Karnak. Who recorded a diplomatic relationship and how did its two courts present it? |
| The Uluburun Shipwreck | Wreckage, copper from Cyprus and glass examined against Amarna finds. What survives of a single cargo and how can its materials be traced? |
| The Stone Heads of San Lorenzo | A carved monument, geological source comparisons and workshop debris. What kinds of evidence reveal the movement and working of heavy stone? |
| Oracle Bones of Anyang | Divination inscriptions, the Fu Hao tomb and evidence of bronze working. What can objects from different contexts reveal about one royal centre? |
| Ugarit Around 1200 BCE | An object from the port, a letter from the city and material from a nearby settlement. What changed at the end of the Late Bronze Age on this part of the coast? |

Each chapter has three selectable map and timeline states. The Amarna place and orientation point are shared by the diplomacy and shipwreck chapters. The archaeological contexts they describe have separate evidence and dates. The investigation of material origins uses independent find and comparison points with published location allowances.
