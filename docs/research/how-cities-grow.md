Tell Brak's coordinate follows Sołtysiak, p. 36. Uruk's point follows the nomination plan's Table 2.1, which gives a centre for the archaeological component. The Arslantepe point follows nomination §1.d. These points approximate site locations and are not building footprints or settlement boundaries. The Uruk and Arslantepe photographs show museum objects. The Uruk mosaic illustrates the technique, with the Commons attribution identifying a temple at Warka rather than an exact building locus. It is attached to the place, not to the exact building record. Media rights and resizing are documented with each local file.

The [Stone Cone Building reconstruction](../../assets/models/uruk-stone-cone-building-reconstruction/asset.json) is a cutaway around 3500 BCE. The export identifies Plan 2.3 (p. 59) and Plan 2.7 (p. 74) as its layout references. The GLB scene includes an initial viewing position and orbit target centred on the courtyard.
