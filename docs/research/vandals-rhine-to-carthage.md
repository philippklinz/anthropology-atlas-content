# Vandals and Alans from the Rhine to Carthage

The chapter follows changing groups into Hispania and Africa, then changes scale to read a private agricultural deed. Its range of 405–496 CE includes both proposed years for the Rhine crossing and the dates of the surviving Albertini archive. The dates describe an editorial selection of episodes. The separate points at Hispalis, Hippo and Carthage identify cities. The crossing places at the Rhine and on both shores of the western Mediterranean remain unspecified.

The two new city locations use the University of Oxford’s RPC Online entry 999 (Carthage) and SIRAR entry 44779 (Hippo Regius). Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places with a 3 km cartographic allowance for the urban area. Hispalis reuses the atlas’s existing Seville place and point. No third-party media are redistributed. Each canonical source record contains its stable link and each event its locator. The texts were compared with the cited passages for this contribution.
