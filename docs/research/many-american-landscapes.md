# The Americas: Many Paths, Many Environments — research draft

23 November 2026. This chapter compares three independent find contexts in the existing Palaeolithic discovery group. Its reading window is **14,700–13,800 calibrated calendar years BP** (before 1950). This range corresponds to the finds selected for the chapter. The older examples from White Sands and the possible routes into the Americas remain in *Across Seas and Land Bridges*. The three stops are ordered for comparison and have no route lines.

The Paisley discussion also uses Shillito et al., Results, Table 1 and Discussion. Lipid biomarkers confirm human origin for three early coprolites. Some of the 21 tested samples have different or ambiguous assignments. The chapter draws no specific diet reconstruction from these results. Kallenbach describes the fibre as likely bulrush, identified as *Schoenoplectus acutus* in her table. Basket and mat are alternative interpretations of the incomplete fragment.

Read material for the restored records includes the Shillito results and tables, Kallenbach's title page, calibration method and date row, Halligan's main article (geological setting, dating, and archaeology), and the Politis main article and Fig. 8. Supplements were not used to establish the displayed ages.

## Monte Verde II

Monte Verde II remains an open dating dispute. This three-site comparison uses its own age evidence and omits Monte Verde as a fixed anchor. Surovell et al. proposed a mid-Holocene reinterpretation drawing on stratigraphy, tephra and redeposition evidence. Dillehay et al. published a rebuttal defending the approximately 14.5 ka date and disputing the tephra correlation and redeposition interpretation. The checkpoint records the USGS/publisher abstract and metadata for the first paper and indexed abstract, argument and concluding passages for the reply. The full papers and supplements would be needed for a separate comparison of both interpretations. This chapter does not adjudicate the dispute.

- Surovell et al. (2026), *Science* 391, 1283–1288. [DOI 10.1126/science.adw9217](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adw9217).
- Dillehay et al. (2026), *PaleoAmerica* 12, 132–143, first published 19 August. [DOI 10.1080/20555563.2026.2699017](https://doi.org/10.1080/20555563.2026.2699017).
