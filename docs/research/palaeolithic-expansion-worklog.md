## Research decisions to preserve

- **Food:** Madjedbebe charred plant remains, Ohalo II plants/tools/fish, Taforalt multi-isotope dietary evidence. Florin 2020, DOI `10.1038/s41467-020-14723-0`, includes special restrictions concerning Mirarr knowledge and imagery; no images, traditional names, recipes or restricted knowledge from that paper are redistributed. The existing Madjedbebe dating dispute remains cited. Ohalo plant processing does not become a claim of established agriculture. Taforalt's isotope graph does not quantify calorie percentages or an exclusively plant diet. Sources include Snir 2015 `10.1371/journal.pone.0131422`, Zohar 2018 `10.1371/journal.pone.0198747`, Moubtahij 2024 `10.1038/s41559-024-02382-z`, Turner 2020 `10.1371/journal.pone.0230642` for Taforalt geography. Zohar's last author is Israel Hershkovitz.
- **Technology:** Sibudu backed pieces and chemical hafting evidence; Fa-Hien Lena bone tools, Phase D 48–34 ka explicitly following read 2019/2020 studies; Xianrendong pottery contexts 20–19 ka, explicitly disputed. No proven Acacia-gum/ochre recipe is claimed from experimental work. Fa-Hien's possible mesh gauge belongs to Phase A 6–4 ka, not the early chapter. Sources include Soriano 2015 `10.1371/journal.pone.0131127`, Chasan 2024 `10.1371/journal.pone.0306402`, Wedage 2019 `10.1038/s41467-019-08623-1`, Langley 2020 `10.1126/sciadv.aba3831`, Wu 2012 `10.1126/science.1218643`, Cohen 2013 and Kuzmin 2015 `10.4312/dp.42.1`. Langley's NC figures are not distributed. The new 2026 Fa-Hien chronology was checked at abstract/preview level, not silently adopted without its full model.
- **Social life:** El Sidrón J1, Shanidar 1, Mtoto at Panga ya Saidi. J1's dental age 7.69 years is separate from its archaeological context. Do not treat the often-quoted 48,400±3,200 radiocarbon BP as calendar BP: the staged event uses an explicitly editorial outer envelope 63,300–43,400 from Davies 2015's two published calibrations, with no invented central date or sigma. Shanidar support is an inference from combined impairments, not a directly observed care relationship. Mtoto's 78,300±4,100 age uncertainty is not a burial duration. No religion, family or personal emotions invented. Sources include Rosas 2017 `10.1126/science.aan6463`, Davies 2015 `10.1016/j.quascirev.2014.12.003`, Trinkaus and Villotte 2017 `10.1371/journal.pone.0186684`, Martinón-Torres 2021 `10.1038/s41586-021-03457-8`. Only modern site photos are redistributed, with CC BY-SA 4.0 credits.
- **Connections:** Enkapune ya Muto and Border Cave bead comparison; Sehonghong bead I13-170 context 33.7–31.9 ka; Ga-Mohana 14.8±0.8 ka manufacture. Miller and Wang 2022 `10.1038/s41586-021-04227-2` stylistic contact is not a literal known route. Stewart 2020 `10.1073/pnas.1921037117` strontium provenance is model-dependent, with minimum distance measured from a study midpoint. Melikane's analysed beads are late Holocene and receive no false 33-ka point. Hatton 2022 `10.1371/journal.pone.0268943` complicates simplistic southern-isolation narratives; Ga-Mohana itself is younger than 19 ka. No invented trade lines.
- **Ice-age life:** Kostenki 11 mammoth-bone circle, Ohalo II huts and Kharaneh IV Structure 1. Kostenki 25,063–24,490 cal BP is context, not a proven roofed house or continuous occupation. Ohalo 23,500–22,500 is a repeated-occupation envelope. Kharaneh's specific published floor bracket is 19,200–18,800 cal BP. No unsupported glacier geometry. Sources include Pryor 2020 `10.15184/aqy.2020.7`, Maher 2012 `10.1371/journal.pone.0031447`, Jones 2016 `10.1016/j.quaint.2015.08.092`, Clark 2009 `10.1126/science.1172873` and reused Ohalo sources.
- **Americas:** Paisley fibre fragment 14,660–14,080 cal BP; Page-Ladson knife context about 14,550; Arroyo Seco 2 horse radius 14,147–13,814. No first-arrival or connected migration claim. Sources include Shillito 2020, Kallenbach 2024 dissertation, Halligan 2016 and Politis 2016 `10.1371/journal.pone.0162870`. Monte Verde is not a fixed anchor: both Surovell 2026 `10.1126/science.adw9217` and the already-published Dillehay 2026 rebuttal `10.1080/20555563.2026.2699017` are documented with access limits. No NC figures copied.
- **Deglaciation:** Gönnersdorf fish-grid engravings, Tahiti coral archive and Shubayqa 1 buildings. Tahiti is explicitly a geological record, not ancient human settlement. The 14,650–14,310 window constrains the pulse; it is not an exactly uniform 340-year rise. The formal Holocene reference converts 11,700 years before 2000 to 11,650 before 1950. Gönnersdorf's coarse coordinate comes from an indexed primary dataset row; direct heiDATA download was blocked and this limitation is documented. Sources include Robitaille 2024 `10.1371/journal.pone.0311302`, Deschamps 2012 `10.1038/nature10902`, Richter 2017 `10.1038/s41598-017-17096-5`, Walker 2009 `10.1002/jqs.1227`, IODP Expedition 310 Hole M0009B.
