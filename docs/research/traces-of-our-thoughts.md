# Traces of Our Thoughts — research notes

Research draft, 2026-11-22. Four bounded examples of early expression; not a comprehensive history of art or a proposed origin date for language, music or cognition. Both language versions are authored summaries. All historical records have source locators.

## Evidence and temporal choices

| Find | Representation | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Blombos L13 | Approximate instant, 73,000 BP | Archaeological layer age; deliberately drawn ochre on silcrete, not engraved ochre and not a deciphered script. Original supplementary analyses and CNRS team account read. |
| Liang Metanduno, motif below LMET2 | Before 67,800 BP | Calcite overgrowth is 71.6 ± 3.8 ka; 67.8 ka is already the conservative lower age bound. Do not subtract its uncertainty twice. Full 2026 paper read; taxonomic authorship remains inferred. |
| Leang Karampuang | Before 51,200 BP | LK1 overlies H2: 53.5 ± 2.3 ka. Dating the whole composition this way follows the authors’ interpretation of it as one scene. Other dated overgrowths yield different minima, not distinct exact painting dates. Full 2024 paper read; it explicitly does not establish a hunting scene. |
| Geißenklösterle flute assemblage | Approximate instant, 40,000 BP | Broad Aurignacian context; the 42–43 ka start of the site sequence is not assigned as a direct date to every flute. Excavators’ site account, 2012 team release and Higham et al.’s institutional abstract read; the full Higham article was not consulted. |

The 80–35 ka initial window is editorial. Open age bounds remain open in the event records. Rounded ages in the papers are placed on the atlas’s fixed 1950 BP comparison scale; this does not imply exact calendar dating or radiocarbon calibration of uranium-series ages. Modern countries locate sites for orientation; they do not classify prehistoric communities.

## Geography and media

Blombos coordinates derive from Henshilwood et al. (2001), Site Background p. 423; Geißenklösterle coordinates from the UNESCO nomination Executive Summary p. 3. The two Indonesian points are deliberately generalized from the primary papers’ Fig. 1 maps, with 20 km editorial accuracy. Karampuang’s reporting summary withholds precise coordinates. These markers are not directions to the caves. No migration, trade or cultural-transmission route is inferred.

Three local images are distributed with their own rights records: the existing Commons PD-Art crop of the Blombos drawing, the authors’ Fig. 3c Karampuang tracing under CC BY 4.0, and Thilo Parg’s flute photograph under CC BY-SA 3.0. The tracing’s JPEG was extracted unchanged from the publisher PDF; surrounding panels, labels and scale bar are not included. Its caption explicitly distinguishes it from an unmodified photograph. The 2026 Muna figures are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and are not bundled. No invented ancient music is supplied.

## Discovery and ownership

Package `early-expression` owns every new record and has no dependencies. Shared concepts were checked before creating identities: the existing uranium-series technology record specifically addresses fossil dating and is not duplicated here. A new explanatory concept addresses minimum ages of images beneath calcite. Chapter membership is explicit and closed over its references.

All four early-human chapters now reuse the `palaeolithic` discovery group. It is an editorial finding aid with no single worldwide boundary; local periodizations remain in the chapter prose. Period cards are removed from the engine explorer; the period dropdown, its explanation, search and chapter cards remain.
