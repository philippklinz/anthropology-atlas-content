BP is represented relative to 1950 using the shared deep-time contract. The chapter's selectable periods are view states, not extra historical period records. No migration is inferred directly from genetic kinship. No arrow connects the Pacific hypothesis to White Sands. No interior ice-free route is drawn as viable at 23 ka.

The migration event type also holds explicitly labelled model scenarios/opportunity windows. These must not be reused as observed journeys or animations. The two Sahul variants are one interpretation group, with A selected initially and a visible comparison mode. Separate findsite events survive route changes.

## Geography

All vector geometry is manually authored from cited factual geography, with unknown spatial accuracy for routes and land areas. It is not traced from third-party map artwork. Point accuracy is an editorial orientation allowance, not a statistical confidence interval.

- Madjedbebe: [132.88, -12.50], rounded from the published 12°30′S, 132°53′E; 2 km allowance.
- White Sands: [-106.32, 32.78], regional park orientation from NPS asset metadata; 20 km allowance, explicitly not an excavation survey. That metadata describes a different NPS photograph from the vendored USGS image.
- Sahul routes: manually placed regional anchors; vertices have no local dates. The northern variant follows the 2017 discussion through Sulawesi/Peleng and Ambon/Seram towards Misool. The southern variant follows Java/Bali and Nusa Tenggara through Timor towards the northwestern shelf.
- Sahul land overlay: only an illustrative part of the Australia–New Guinea connection around today's Torres Strait. Its outline is not a palaeocoastline digitized from the study. The original research figure is available separately, with its own legend.
- Beringia: partial land-connection marker split at ±180°, as is the coastal hypothesis. The component split is technical, not an additional event.

Land assertions carry one snapshot date each. Between snapshots the viewer does not morph or extrapolate a coastline. Modern land remains visible as explicitly labelled orientation. No ice-sheet polygon was added without appropriately licensed, checked geometry.

## Distributed media

- `wallacea-65ka-model`: unchanged embedded JPEG from Kealy et al. 2017, Fig. 3, 1267 × 887. The institutional PDF cover explicitly grants CC BY 4.0; authors credited. Blue buffers depict intervisibility, not sea depth or migration probabilities. Figure 1's undated −50 m shading was deliberately not used as a 65 ka snapshot.
- `white-sands-footprints`: original NPS photograph distributed by USGS, copied unchanged. USGS explicitly states Public Domain. Both media files have independent rights and source records and are delivered locally.

Other papers are bibliographic links only. In particular, no CC BY-NC-ND artwork from the Beringia papers is redistributed or adapted into GPL geometry.
