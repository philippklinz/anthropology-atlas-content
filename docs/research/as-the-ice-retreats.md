# As the ice retreats · Research notes

The 19,000–11,650 BP viewing window places three independent examples alongside a geological reference. BP uses the atlas epoch of 1950. Points represent two archaeological sites and a modern offshore coring location; there are no interpolated ancient coastlines or migration paths.

| Example | Encoding | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gönnersdorf, Plaquette 282 | Approximate site context, 15,800 BP | Robitaille et al. 2024, Introduction, pp. 4–6, Fig. 9 and Discussion pp. 14–15. Reflectance imaging and the comparison of motifs support an interpretation of net fishing. The site age does not directly date an individual incision. Shown marks come from engraving, alongside the research team's tracing. [Paper and figure](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311302) |
| Tiarei offshore Tahiti | Study window, 14,650–14,310 BP | Deschamps et al. 2012, pp. 559–561 and Fig. 2 constrain Meltwater Pulse 1A. The bounds summarize the earliest proposed onset and latest end; they do not state a uniform 340-year period. The range 12–22 m (most probably 14–18 m) is local relative sea-level rise at Tahiti. A single representative IODP borehole orients within the multi-core data. [Deschamps et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10902) · [IODP record](https://sedis.iodp.org/exp-catalogue/?urn=urn%3Aorg.iodp%3Aexp%3A310%3Asite%3AM0009%3Ahole%3AB) |
| Shubayqa 1, Structure 1 and early sequence | Modelled age envelope, 14,600–14,000 cal BP | Richter et al. 2017, p. 2 and pp. 3–4, gives coordinates, architectural features and the 2σ modelled window for Phases 7–4. The window spans several archaeological phases, not a single structure's lifespan. Later phases follow gaps. The 2σ label describes a reported bracket; the atlas does not assert symmetry or derive a central age. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-17096-5) |
| Geological Holocene boundary | Rounded age 11,650 BP | Walker et al. 2009, Abstract and Summary, gives 11,700 calendar years before 2000 with a maximum counting error of 99 years. The reference-year difference is 50 years. This age defines a geological boundary. [Published PDF](https://stratigraphy.org/gssps/files/holocene.pdf) |

Gönnersdorf's point [7.42, 50.45] rounds the Late Upper Palaeolithic localities K I–IV in Siemssen and Maier's 2024 heiDATA V1 Supplement A, section 5, rows 10–13. The restoration retrieved the PDF and checked these rows directly. The point uses an editorial 2 km tolerance. Tiarei uses the coordinate of IODP 310 Hole M0009B, [−149.4034, −17.48858], as an offshore place distinct from the island of Tahiti elsewhere in the dataset. Shubayqa uses the rounded article coordinates [37.2281, 32.4064] from p. 2. [Site table](https://doi.org/10.11588/DATA/ZUJRXV)
