# Life at the edge of the ice · Research notes

The 30,000–18,800 BP viewing window covers three archaeological examples and a global ice-history reference. All ages encoded as BP use the 1950 epoch. In this chapter each point locates a published site or site area on the present-day map.

| Evidence | Encoding | Source and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kostenki 11, third mammoth-bone structure | Uncertain instant, 25,063–24,490 cal BP | Pryor et al. 2020, Table 2, combined range of five coherent ages (95.4%, IntCal13). The Archaeological context section supplies 51°23′08″N, 39°03′05″E, rounded to [39.0514, 51.3856]. The 12.5 m circle and evidence of wood and bone fuel are recorded. The Discussion considers dwelling and storage without settling the function. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.15184/aqy.2020.7) |
| Ohalo II brush huts | First-to-last evidence envelope, 23,500–22,500 cal BP | Snir et al. 2015, Materials and Methods, The Ohalo II site and Fig. 1 describe six brush hut features, sunken floors and the materials identified in Brush Hut 1. Zohar et al. 2018, Background, The Ohalo II site gives the rounded age range across repeated visits and describes successive floors. The span does not imply continuous use. [Snir et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0131422) · [Zohar et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198747) |
| Kharaneh IV, Structure 1 | Uncertain instant, 19,200–18,800 cal BP | Maher et al. 2012, pp. 2–3 and Fig. 3 give the approximately 3.2 × 2.2 m floor, the burned layer and the bracket from samples below and above the floor (68%, IntCal09). This is the published bracket for Structure 1. The abstract uses a rounded date for the huts and the full occupation sequence is broader. Jones et al. 2016, accepted manuscript pp. 7–8, describe wetland sediments and nearby sections around [36.45, 31.72], used for site orientation with a 2 km tolerance. [Maher et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0031447) · [Jones et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quaint.2015.08.092) |
| Last Glacial Maximum | Rounded context interval, 26,500–19,000 BP | Clark et al. 2009, Abstract and pp. 710–711 describe near-maximum extent for most ice sheets between 26.5 and 19–20 ka. The time bar summarizes a global synthesis with regional variation. There is no glacial polygon in the package. [Author abstract and publication record](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70036965) |

The cost of an exact-looking map point matters here. Tolerances in the geometry metadata are editorial estimates. At Kharaneh IV the point approximates the site area from the sediment sections and does not locate the excavated floor. The Ohalo II site and point are reused from `package:early-foodways` alongside the two source records. Each event and findsite assertion gives its context and references. All new records are drafts.

The sole image is the publisher’s unchanged large web PNG of Snir et al. (2015), Fig. 1, 1063 × 848 pixels, under CC BY 4.0. It shows the central excavation area and a locator map. Credit and origin are recorded with the local asset. [Figure](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0131422.g001) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
